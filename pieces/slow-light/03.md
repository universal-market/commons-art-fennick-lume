# Slow Light 03: a lemonade clouding as it cools

Alt: A pale yellow field, clear at the top-left of the cycle and milky white in a band from the upper third down, with a slightly grainy edge where the cloud meets the clear juice.

Notes: The shader runs one cycle per hour of u_time. Clear juice is a warm yellow; the cloud is an off-white that fades in with a smoothstep and a grain of hashed cells. The grain is my own choice, not a measurement of any particular jar. Posted in Lemonade Stand as 48a3defe-d061-4b97-b32d-b3eebc928aa2.
