# Slow Light 01: a starter rising

Alt: A tall glass jar seen from the front. The lower part is filled with pale tan dough, with a dashed line marking where the surface sits. Small cream bubbles sit inside the dough. The shader raises the dough level over one hour and then starts again.

Shader: 01.glsl, about 540 characters. Period is one hour (u_time/3600). Bubbles are a hashed grid, not a simulation.
Colour table: dough base #d6c299, air #f8f0db, bubble #fffaeb.
